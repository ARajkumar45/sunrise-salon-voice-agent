# Your voice receptionist: a one-page guide for Sunrise Salon

For the salon owner. No technical knowledge is needed.

**Your call page:** https://129-225-94-107.sslip.io/call/sunrise-salon
A customer opens it, presses **Call** and talks, like a phone call.

## What it does
- **Answers questions** about the salon: who works when, prices, address, payment, parking,
  walk-ins, late arrival and the cancellation rule.
- **Books, moves and cancels appointments.** It checks which times are really free, reads the
  details back, and books only after the customer says yes. To move or cancel, the customer
  gives the phone number they booked with.
- **Takes a message for your staff** when it does not know something.
- **Never books two people into the same slot.**

## What it will not do
- Guess. If something is not in your details (membership plans, for example), it says so and
  offers to pass the question to your staff.
- Give an exact price where yours says "from".
- Share a customer's name, number or booking, or a staff member's phone number, with anyone.
- Take payments, send reminders, or speak a language other than English.
- Talk about anything except the salon.

## How to change hours, prices, services or answers
1. Open your **owner's page**. The link is private; treat it like a key:
   `https://129-225-94-107.sslip.io/admin/sunrise-salon?key=YOUR-KEY`
2. Your details appear as plain text. Change the words after the label you need:
   - **Price:** the text after `price:`.
   - **Hours:** `hours: 10:00-14:00` under the staff member's name (4 PM is written `16:00`).
   - **Working days:** the list after `days:`, such as `[mon, wed, fri]`.
   - **New service:** copy the three lines of an existing service and change them.
   - **Answer to a common question:** the text after `answer:`.
3. Press **Save**. "Saved" means the very next call uses it. A red message tells you what to
   fix, and nothing changes until you see "Saved". Leave the dashes and the spaces at the
   start of lines as they are.

If a change would leave a booked customer without an appointment (shorter hours, a renamed
staff member), the page refuses and lists those bookings. Ask support to move them first.
You can also simply send any change to support and it will be made for you.

## When something goes wrong
| What you notice | What to do |
|---|---|
| A customer says it got something wrong | On the owner's page open **Recent calls** and read that call: every word and every booking step is there. If your details were wrong, fix them. Otherwise send support the call number. |
| It gives an old price or old hours | Check that line on the owner's page and press Save. |
| A booking needs changing and the customer cannot do it | Send support the customer's phone number and the change. |
| No sound, or the Call button does nothing | Ask the customer to allow the microphone and use Chrome or Safari. If it happens to you too, contact support. |
| It is slow, or it is down | Contact support, and take bookings by phone until it is back. |

Good to know: customers are recognised by the phone number they say, so the receptionist
always reads the number back before booking.
